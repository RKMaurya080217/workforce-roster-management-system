3. ROOT CAUSE & ARCHITECTURAL FIX SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED & PERMANENTLY FIXED]
1. Issue 1 - Weekly Off Assigned Before Night:
   - Root Cause: `planWeeklyOffs` in 5-day mode did not reserve 0 offs for Sunday night
     workers or <=1 off for Saturday night workers, and `pairTemplates` lacked `mFirstNight`
     ordering checks. Furthermore, `enforceAndRepairExactWeeklyOff` could strip a mandatory
     Monday recovery off (following Sunday night of previous cycle) when checking `firstNightDate`,
     and `evaluateFinalValidation` didn't deduct deferred offs in 5-day mode.
   - Solution: Enforced strict Night-First commitment in `generateDay`, updated `planWeeklyOffs`
     and `enforceAndRepairExactWeeklyOff` for both 5-day and 6-day modes, protected previous-cycle
     Sunday night Monday recovery offs (`!postNightOffDates.contains(...)` and cross-cycle checks
     in `rebalanceDailyCoverage`), and synchronized `evaluateFinalValidation` target calculations.
2. Issue 2 - Development Team Night Preferences (Divyansh & Sriram):
   - Root Cause: `planNightSchedule`, `repairDailyCoverage`, and `solveDay` unconditionally
     excluded `isDevelopmentTeam()` employees from NIGHT shifts, overriding explicit NIGHT preferences.
   - Solution: Added rule-based `hasNightPreference` / `hasNightPreferenceForDate` helpers (zero
     hardcoded names in production logic) so explicitly preferring Night enables eligibility while
     non-preferring Dev Team members remain Mon-Fri General.
3. Issue 3 - Regeneration Diversity After Draft Deletion:
   - Root Cause: Deleted draft history needed immediate flush (`saveAndFlush`), candidate solver
     permutations needed tie-breaking perturbation across eligible pools (`cycleSalt`), and 5-day
     validation false positives prevented clean candidate selection.
   - Solution: Flushed deletion history, added salt-based permutation tie-breaker in `planNightSchedule`,
     and resolved 5-day validation conflicts so distinct, zero-conflict candidates are chosen.
