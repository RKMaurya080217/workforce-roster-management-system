3. ROOT CAUSE & ARCHITECTURAL FIX SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED]
1. Issue 1 — Wrong Weekly Off Order (Off Preceding Night):
   - In 6-day mode, the generator pre-assigned Weekly Off on Saturday for an employee working Sunday night,
     placing Weekly Off BEFORE the Night shift in the same roster cycle.
   - Core Invariant: NIGHT SHIFT -> IMMEDIATE NEXT-DAY WEEKLY OFF. An off prior to a later night shift violates
     recovery sequencing. For Sunday night workers in 6-day mode, Sunday night must be followed by Monday OFF
     of the subsequent roster cycle (resulting in 0 offs in the current Monday-Sunday cycle).
2. Issue 2 — Day-Wise Shift Preference Not Honored:
   - Configured Saturday Night preferences (e.g., Rajat Maurya) were overwritten by candidate scoring penalties
     or daytime balance heuristics where assigning Weekly Off received 0 penalty while night transitions incurred
     penalties.
   - General profile avoidance (`isShiftAvoided(NIGHT)`) was incorrectly overriding specific date preferences.
3. Capacity Calculation & Development Team Safety:
   - When active male staffing was <= 4, individual `maxNightsPerWeek` caps below 2 physically precluded covering
     7 nights in a 7-day week (4 * 1 = 4 < 7). Heuristic boosting to 2 nights ensured mathematical feasibility.
   - Development team members were strictly exempted from Night shift rotations across all backtracking and
     scoring branches.
