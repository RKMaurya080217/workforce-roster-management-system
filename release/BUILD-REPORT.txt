3. ROOT CAUSE & ARCHITECTURAL FIX SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ROOT CAUSE IDENTIFIED]
1. Infeasibility Reporting:
   - When minimum required workforce capacity was mathematically insufficient (e.g. fewer active employees than
     required daily coverage + mandatory weekly offs, or 4 employees with 2 females where 2 males cannot physically
     cover 7 nights under max-2-night limits), the engine threw standard exceptions rather than the explicit
     "ROSTER INFEASIBLE / WORKFORCE CAPACITY SHORTAGE" required by operations.
2. Evening Shift Rebalance Drift:
   - In `pickBestWorkShiftForEmployee`, when an employee's Weekly Off was repaired or reallocated, the fallback
     shift defaulted to `ShiftType.EVENING`, creating an over-allocation of evening shifts (> 2 max capacity)
     resulting in `MAX_COVERAGE_EVENING` warnings and conflicts.
3. Mid-Week Partial-Week Eligibility:
   - Employees joining mid-week or resigning mid-week were incorrectly evaluated for a full 6-day work week
     by `RosterValidatorService`, generating false `INVALID_WORKING_DAYS_COUNT` violations.
4. Historical Cycle Preservation:
   - Weekly off repair across week boundaries (`enforceAndRepairExactWeeklyOff`) lacked a lower bound guard,
     risking mutating previous cycle day assignments when repairing Day 1.
5. Sunday Night Cross-Week Off Validation:
   - Cross-week transition checks in `RosterValidatorService` exempted employees if their start date was not OFF,
     permitting illegal Monday morning shifts immediately following Sunday night.
6. Null Safety for OFF Shift Intervals:
   - `ShiftType.OFF` has null `startTime` and `endTime`. Evaluating rest intervals against `OFF` threw
     `NullPointerException: time` inside `LocalDate.atTime`.
